# Power BI Dashboard User Guide

## Page 1: Executive Overview Dashboard
* **Header KPIs:** Displays **Total Bookings** (150K), **Total Revenue** ($51.85M), **Completed Rides** (93K), **Avg Customer Rating** (4.40), and **Avg Driver Rating** (4.23).
* **Booking Status Breakdown:** Visualizes the proportion of completed vs. cancelled vs. incomplete rides using a Donut Chart / KPI cards.
* **Revenue & Distance Trends:** Line/Bar charts tracking booking values over time.

## Page 2: Cancellation & Operational Analysis
* **Customer Cancellation Breakdown:** Bar chart displaying top customer reasons (*Wrong Address*, *Change of Plans*, *Driver Not Moving*).
* **Driver Cancellation Breakdown:** Bar chart detailing driver cancellation causes (*Customer issues*, *Sick customer*, *Car issues*).
* **Unmatched Demand & Incomplete Trips:** Dedicated visual tracking *No Driver Found* and *Incomplete Ride* volumes.

## Page 3: Route & Fleet Intelligence
* **Location Analysis:** Matrices and horizontal bar charts showing top 10 pickup and drop-off hotspots.
* **Vehicle Type Performance:** Table/Chart displaying total trips, revenue generated, and average rating broken down by vehicle category (Auto, Mini, Sedan, XL).
* **Payment Method Distribution:** Pie chart breaking down payment preference across Cash, UPI, Wallet, and Cards.

## Interactivity & Slicers
* **Date Range Slicer:** Filters metrics dynamically across days, weeks, or months.
* **Vehicle Type Slicer:** Allows focused analysis on specific fleet categories.
* **Booking Status Slicer:** Isolates completed, cancelled, or unfulfilled ride segments.
