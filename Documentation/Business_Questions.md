# Core Business Questions & Strategic Focus Areas

## 1. Volume & Revenue Performance
* What is the total volume of bookings processed, and what percentage converts to completed rides?
* How much total revenue is generated, and what is the average revenue per completed booking?
* What is the total mileage logged across all completed trips?

## 2. Cancellation Mechanics & Revenue Leakage
* What percentage of bookings fail to complete, and how are they split across customer cancellations, driver cancellations, incomplete rides, and "No Driver Found"?
* What are the top reported reasons for customer cancellations?
* What primary reasons lead drivers to cancel accepted bookings?

## 3. Geographical & Route Analysis
* Which pickup locations exhibit the highest ride demand?
* Which drop locations receive the most traffic volume?
* Are there specific location pairs that experience higher cancellation rates?

## 4. Vehicle & Fleet Optimization
* How is ride volume distributed across different vehicle types (Auto, Go Mini, Go Sedan, Uber XL, etc.)?
* Which vehicle type generates the highest average revenue per trip?

## 5. Service Quality & Satisfaction
* What are the overall average ratings for customers and drivers?
* How do customer ratings vary by vehicle type or booking status?
