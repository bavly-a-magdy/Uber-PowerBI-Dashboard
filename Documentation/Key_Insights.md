# Key Analytical Insights & Executive Findings

## 1. Booking Conversion & Revenue Loss
* **Overall Completion Rate:** **62.0%** (93,000 rides completed out of 150,000 total requests).
* **Gross Revenue Generated:** **$51.85 Million** across completed rides, with an average fare of **$557.49** per completed trip.
* **Lost Demand:** **38.0%** (57,000 requests) resulted in lost bookings due to cancellations or system shortfalls.

## 2. Drivers of Cancellation
* **Driver Cancellations (27,000 rides / 47.4% of lost bookings):** Primary reasons include *Customer-related issues*, *Customer ill/coughing*, *Personal & Car-related issues*, and *Exceeding passenger limits*.
* **Customer Cancellations (10,500 rides / 18.4% of lost bookings):** Driven by *Wrong Address entered*, *Change of plans*, *Driver not moving towards pickup location*, and *Driver asking to cancel*.
* **Unfulfilled Requests (10,500 rides):** Accounted for "No Driver Found" occurrences due to localized supply constraints.
* **Incomplete Rides (9,000 rides):** Terminated mid-trip due to technical or vehicle breakdowns.

## 3. High-Density Geographic Nodes
* **Top Pickup Locations:** *Khandsa* (949 bookings), *Barakhamba Road* (946 bookings), and *Saket* (931 bookings).
* **Top Drop Locations:** *Ashram* (936 bookings), *Basai Dhankot* (917 bookings), and *Lok Kalyan Marg* (916 bookings).

## 4. Quality & Rating Metrics
* **Customer Satisfaction:** Overall average customer rating stands at **4.40 / 5.00**.
* **Driver Satisfaction:** Overall average driver rating stands at **4.23 / 5.00**.
